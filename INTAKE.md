# Intake — what I need from you

Everything built so far is *coherent*. This is what makes it *correct*.

Answer in any format — inline, a voice note transcript, a messy paragraph. Don't
tidy it up; I'd rather have rough truth than polished approximation. Anything you
don't know, write "don't know" — that's a real answer and it changes what I
recommend.

**Ordered by what it unblocks.** Tier 1 answers change the whole strategy. Tier 3
just sharpens it.

---

## Tier 1 — blocking. Everything downstream depends on these.

### 1. Does any of this exist yet?

I've written a *growth* plan because you said growth stage. If the studio and
publication aren't actually operating, it's a *launch* plan, and it's a
substantially different document.

- **1.1** Does 7th Creation have paying clients today? Roughly how many in the last
  12 months?
- **1.2** Does the publication exist — anything published, anywhere? Or is it
  planned?
- **1.3** Is there a website? URL, or "not yet".
- **1.4** How long has the studio been running?

### 2. The numbers

Nothing about raising prices can be sized without these. Ranges are fine.

- **2.1** What do you charge now? Day rate, typical project value, or retainer value
- **2.2** Roughly what share of proposals do you win? *(above ~70% is the strongest
  single signal that you're underpriced)*
- **2.3** What share of new business is inbound vs. you going out to find it?
- **2.4** What share of revenue is retained/repeat vs. one-off projects?
- **2.5** Biggest and smallest engagement in the last year

### 3. Who else bids for these briefs?

Flagged three times now as the biggest gap. The positioning claims a territory,
and I can't verify it's unclaimed without knowing who's standing next to you.

- **3.1** Name 3–5 studios or agencies you actually lose work to, or get compared to
- **3.2** When you lose, what do you lose to? Price, scope, relationship, or someone
  simply being better known?
- **3.3** Is there a studio you'd point to and say "that's the level we want"?

### 4. Where do you operate?

Affects the competitive set, the register the publication writes in, and the
jurisdiction for the Day 7 trademark search.

- **4.1** Where are you based?
- **4.2** Where are your clients — local, national, international?

## Tier 2 — needed for the next deliverables

### 5. Day 7 — the productised offer

`brand/architecture.md` §03.3 specifies the shape; these fill it.

- **5.1** What exactly would a creator get? List the deliverables
- **5.2** How long does that take you, realistically, start to finish?
- **5.3** What would you charge? *(Set from delivery cost + margin — not by
  discounting the studio rate. If it's a discount, the separation fails.)*
- **5.4** How many could you deliver a month without it eating studio capacity?

### 6. Publishing the editorial standards

The three documents in `editorial/` are drafted and can't go live without these.

- **6.1** An email or route for people to report errors and disclosure failures
- **6.2** Where will the correction log live? **It has to exist before the policy
  claiming it does goes live**
- **6.3** Sponsorship — do you take any today? Is it material revenue? *(The plan
  recommends declining it; if it's already a real line, that recommendation has a
  cost and you should overrule me.)*
- **6.4** Does anyone involved hold a financial stake in a company you'd cover?
- **6.5** Are you willing to publicly commit to *never* letting a client see a piece
  before publication? **Say no if it's a no** — the policy is worthless if it
  breaks the first time a big client asks.

### 7. The logo

`brand/identity-brief.md` is ready to brief a designer; these complete it.

- **7.1** Do vector source files exist, or only the images you sent? *(If it was
  generated rather than drawn, say so — it changes the redraw approach, and it's
  not a problem, just a fact I need.)*
- **7.2** What typeface is the wordmark? If unknown, send the source file
- **7.3** The exact hex values for the gold and orange — mine are eyeballed
- **7.4** Who'll do the redraw? You, a designer you have, or someone to be found?

## Tier 3 — sharpens the work

### 8. Capacity

- **8.1** How many people, and who does what?
- **8.2** Realistically, how often could you publish? *(An under-fed media arm is
  worse than none — it advertises inconsistency to the buyers you're trying to
  impress.)*
- **8.3** Who writes? You, or is that a hire?

### 9. Proof

- **9.1** 2–3 pieces of work you're proudest of, and why
- **9.2** Any client you could name publicly, with an outcome
- **9.3** Has anyone ever hired you *because* of something you published? Even once
  — it's the proof point the whole model rests on

### 10. Constraints

- **10.1** Anything you've already decided that I should stop questioning?
- **10.2** Any work you won't take — sectors, client types, project types?
- **10.3** Budget for identity, site, or publishing over the next 12 months?
