> **7th Creation is a creative studio that earns its work by publishing what it

One idea. Everything below is this sentence at a different altitude.

## 02 — Value Proposition

7th Creation is a creative studio for people building in the digital world. Rather
than pitching, we publish: research into our field that is sourced, dated and
revisited when the facts change. By the time a client talks to us they have already
watched us reason through the exact problems they're facing — so the conversation
starts at "when can you start", not "convince me you're good".

## 03 — Tagline and Positioning Line

**These are two different jobs, and the brand needs both.**

A **descriptor** says what you are, for someone who has never heard of you. A
**positioning line** says why you're different. Conflating them produces a line that
does neither.

### The descriptor — keep it

> **A Creative Studio**

Already set in the logo lockup. It is doing its job correctly: plain, immediately
legible, no work required from the reader. **Don't replace it.** A cold prospect
needs to know what you are before they can care why you're different.

### The positioning line — add it

For the site, decks, campaigns and proposals. Not in the logo.

| Line | Style | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **We show our work.** ⭐ | Functional / double-meaning | Says three things at once: the maker's habit of showing working, the literal fact of publishing in public, and the rigour underneath both. Short enough to survive anywhere |
| Proof before pitch. | Functional | The mechanism, stated bluntly. Sharp, slightly combative |
| The thinking, in public. | Descriptive | Clearest explanation of the media arm; weakest as a line |
| Hired for what we've already published. | Explanatory | Says the model precisely, but too long to work as a line |
| Verified, not viral. | Witty | Was the recommendation under the media-brand model. Still good *for the publication* — retain it as the editorial standard's line, not the studio's |

**Recommended: "We show our work."**

The double meaning is doing real work. To a designer it is the discipline of
exposing your process; to a client it is the promise that nothing is asserted
without evidence; and to a reader it is the literal description of the publication.
One line, three audiences, no contradiction between them — which is exactly what
this brand needs given it has to hold two engines at once.

It also has the property a good positioning line needs: **it can be broken.** If the
studio stopped publishing, the line would become a lie. Lines that can't be violated
can't be believed.

## 04 — Messaging Hierarchy

**Level 1 — Brand Headline**

> We show our work.

**Level 2 — Supporting Statement**

> 7th Creation is a creative studio for people building in the digital world. We
> publish our research and reasoning in the open — sourced, dated, and corrected
> when we're wrong — so you can judge how we think before you hire us.

**Level 3 — Key Messages (four proof pillars)**

**1. You can read our thinking before you hire us.**
The publication isn't marketing. It is the studio working in public — the research,
the options rejected, the trade-off taken.
- *Proof:* The published archive. Pieces that show reasoning, not just outcomes.

**2. Every claim is sourced and dated.**
Traceable to where it came from and when it was true. Nothing runs on "reportedly".
- *Proof:* Public sourcing standard. Inline citation. Visible last-verified dates.

**3. We go back and fix it.**
Publishing isn't the end of the obligation. When facts change, the piece changes,
and the change is logged.
- *Proof:* Public correction log. Changelogs on evergreen pieces.

**4. We're selective about the work we take.**
A studio that is evidently choosing its clients is a different proposition from one
that is evidently available.
- *Proof:* 🔶 A stated point of view on what you do and don't take on.

> Pillars 2 and 3 are the two nobody in the category does consistently, both are
> cheap to build, and both are hard to adopt credibly after the fact. Ship them
> first — `marketing/growth-plan.md`, Weeks 3–4.

## 05 — Messaging by Audience

### Startups — brand & identity *(primary)*

- **Lead with:** clarity and speed of getting to a defensible position.
- **Say:** "You'll leave knowing what you stand for and why it's different — and
  with the reasoning written down, so it survives your next hire."
- **Don't say:** anything implying a long discovery phase. This buyer is
  time-poor and allergic to agency theatre.

### Brands — content & campaigns *(primary)*

- **Lead with:** judgement, not capacity. They can buy execution anywhere.
- **Say:** "We'll tell you when we think the brief is wrong. That's what you're
  paying for."
- **Don't say:** volume or turnaround promises. Competing on throughput puts you
  next to production shops on price.

### Enterprise *(graduate into)*

- **Lead with:** rigour and defensibility. This buyer must justify the choice
  internally.
- **Say:** "Everything we recommend is documented, sourced and defensible to your
  stakeholders."
- **Don't say:** anything about being small, scrappy or fast-moving. Reassurance
  beats energy here.

### Creators — via **Day 7** *(productised)*

- **Lead with:** the fixed shape. Price, scope and timeline, stated up front.
- **Say:** "Fixed price, fixed scope, done by [date]."
- **Always endorsed:** "Day 7 by 7th Creation" — never the name alone.
  See `brand/architecture.md` §03.2 for why this one matters.
- **Don't say:** anything that invites custom scoping. The offer's discipline *is*
  its message. See `brand/architecture.md` §3.

### Readers of the publication

- **Lead with:** the time saved and the exposure removed.
- **Say:** "You can act on this and still defend it in three months."
- **Don't say:** "insights", "must-read", "curated". This audience discounts those
  words automatically.

### Contributors and hires

- **Lead with:** the standard. Practitioners are drawn to the constraint.
- **Say:** "We publish less than everyone else and check more than anyone else."

## 06 — Elevator Pitches

**5 seconds**
> A creative studio. We publish our thinking, and people hire us from it.

**15 seconds**
> 7th Creation is a creative studio for people building digital products. We
> publish research into our own field — sourced and dated — so clients can see how
> we think before they hire us. Most of our work comes from people who read
> something first.

**60 seconds**
> Most studios sell on a portfolio and a pitch. The problem with both is that
> they show finished work, and finished work tells you almost nothing about how a
> team reasons — which is the thing you're actually buying.
>
> So we do it the other way round. We publish our research and our reasoning in the
> open: what we found, what we rejected, what we still don't know. Everything is
> sourced and dated, and when we're wrong we correct it in public.
>
> That means by the time someone talks to us, they've already watched us work
> through the problems they have. The pitch is redundant. It also means we're
> selective — we publish a point of view, and it filters out the clients who want
> something else. That's deliberate.

## 07 — Proof Point Bank

Ranked by weight. 🔶 must be **true and verifiable before use** — a brand built on
rigour cannot make an unverified claim about its own rigour.

| Claim | Type | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The published archive itself | Structural | 🔶 Ongoing — the core asset |
| Public sourcing standard | Structural | ✅ Drafted — `editorial/sourcing-standard.md` |
| Public correction log | Structural | ✅ Policy drafted; the log itself must be built before publishing it |
| Case studies showing reasoning | Credential | 🔶 Build — Weeks 3–4 |
| Named client outcomes | Commercial | 🔶 Gather |
| % of clients who read first | Commercial | 🔶 Instrument — Weeks 1–2 |
| External citations | Third-party | 🔶 Track from day one |
| Client repeat / retainer rate | Commercial | 🔶 Measure |

**The strongest proof point available to this brand is the last one it can fake:**
the percentage of clients who read the publication before they made contact. Nobody
else in the category can quote that number, because nobody else has the mechanism.
Instrument it early — `growth-plan.md` Weeks 1–2.
