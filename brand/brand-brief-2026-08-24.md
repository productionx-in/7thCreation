## 01 — What the business is

7th Creation is a **multidisciplinary creative studio**, currently working across:

- Events
- Advertising
- Films / video production
- Marketing
- Social media management
- Content creation
- Web design & development

**Long-term vision:** grow into a larger film and production studio. **But the
current identity must not feel like a conventional film-production company.** It
should feel like a creative studio first — cinematic, imaginative, premium,
contemporary and versatile.

The brand needs to communicate *"we can create anything"* — campaigns, films,
events, digital experiences, websites, social content, and eventually larger
productions — without the homepage reading as a list of services.

## 02 — The brand concept

The name draws on the idea of the **"7th Wonder"** and the creation of a new world.

**Logo symbolism, precise:**
- The **7** represents creation and the identity of 7th Creation
- The **C** is integrated with the 7 and resembles a crescent, an eclipse, a new
  world
- Inside the C, a small human figure stands on a mountain
- The figure represents **vision, ambition**, the creator looking toward a new
  world
- The mountains represent **exploration, journey, limitless possibilities**
- The celestial element represents **imagination, ideas, possibility**
- Together: **"creating a new world through creativity and vision"**

**Non-negotiable:** the 7 and the C must always read as one unified symbol. Never
visually separate them, never let the mark read as a literal "7 + C." Cinematic,
but never cliché.

## 03 — Brand personality

**Is:** Creative · Cinematic · Imaginative · Premium · Modern · Confident ·
Experimental · Sophisticated · Human · Visionary

**Is not:** Corporate · Generic digital agency · Overly luxurious · Cartoonish ·
Gaming-related · Too futuristic/tech-heavy · A traditional Bollywood/Hollywood
production house

**Do not use all six equally.** Gold and orange are accents, not foundations —
their entire power depends on scarcity. Flat colours for reproducibility (print,
embroidery, signage); metallic gradients are a special-occasion finish only.

## 05 — Typography

Sophisticated modern sans-serif as primary. Clean, spacious, editorial. Build
personality through hierarchy and spacing, not decorative type.

**Avoid:** overly trendy fonts, futuristic fonts, excessive rounded fonts, generic
startup typography.

🔶 **Not yet specified: which typeface.** Needs selection against this brief —
see `identity-brief.md` §06, which currently proposes a serif/sans pairing that
predates this brief and should be re-evaluated against "sophisticated modern
sans-serif as the primary typeface" above.

## 06 — Logo system required

The locked logo is the primary identity. Maintain the proportions and relationship
between the 7, crescent/C, figure, landscape and celestial element across every
version:

1. Full-colour logo
2. Dark-background version
3. Light-background version
4. Single-colour black version
5. Single-colour white version
6. Simplified icon/mark
7. Small-size/favicon version

Must work from a favicon and Instagram profile picture all the way to film title
cards, billboards, business cards and production credits, and always read as the
same brand across that whole range.

**Built-in flexibility requirement:** the identity must be able to move from

> 7TH CREATION
> A CREATIVE STUDIO

to

> 7TH CREATION
> FILMS

later, **without a full rebrand.** Practical consequence: the master mark and the
tagline/descriptor must stay cleanly separable — never bake "A CREATIVE STUDIO"
into the lockup as one fused unit.

## 07 — Website direction

**The core instruction, stated twice for emphasis:**

> "These people create worlds and experiences" — not "We offer 7 different
> services."

Feel like a creative studio portfolio, not a conventional agency site. Visually
driven, cinematic, editorial. Strong typography, large imagery/video, generous
negative space, intentional motion, unusual but controlled layouts.

**Explicitly to avoid:** glassmorphism, generic gradients, floating cards,
unnecessary rounded containers, stock-agency layouts, excessive animation,
template-based creative-agency aesthetics.

> "Think like a creative director designing a studio's digital identity, not a
> developer assembling UI components. Every section should have a reason to exist."

## 08 — ⚠️ What this means for `site/` as it stands

Stated plainly: **the current build in `site/` does not meet this brief**, and
that gap should be visible rather than papered over.

It was built by reusing interaction patterns from a MotionSites "3D Creator"
portfolio template — rounded containers throughout, a fairly conventional
hero/marquee/about/services/projects/process/contact structure, card-based
sections. That is close to the exact silhouette §07 asks to avoid.

**What's been corrected so far:** the colour palette now matches this brief
exactly (§04, applied in the same session this brief arrived). Structure, layout,
motion language and the logo system are **not yet addressed.**

**This is a real scope decision, not a small follow-on.** Meeting §06 and §07
properly means: commissioning the actual logo system (7 variants, from a
professional redraw — see `identity-brief.md`), and very likely rebuilding the
site's structure and art direction rather than reskinning the current one. Flagged
here rather than taken on silently — see the open decisions in `INTAKE.md` and the
founder's own steer on how much of that to do now versus sequence later.
